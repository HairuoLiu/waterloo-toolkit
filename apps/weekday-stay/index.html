① Executive summary

一句话结论：拓展到 Kitchener（含 Downtown）、Cambridge、Guelph 后，最便宜、最易触「梦想 $500/月」的，是 Kitchener / Cambridge 的预算汽车旅馆（带周价/小厨房） 与 KW 地区家具私人房 两条赛道；延长住宿酒店（Staybridge / Homewood / Home2）仍是「酒店体验 + 可谈价」的最佳平衡点。

Bottom line: once you expand to Kitchener (incl. downtown), Cambridge and Guelph, the cheapest routes to the "dream $500/month" are budget motels in Kitchener / Cambridge with weekly rates and kitchenettes and furnished private rooms in the KW area. Extended-stay hotels (Staybridge / Homewood / Home2) remain the best balance of "hotel experience + negotiable rate".

⑦ Pricing strategy

谈判锚点（不主动报底价）

  • 开局（Round 1）： 让对方先报「13 周固定、每周二三相隔、同房号」的企业/长住价。绝不先说 $500/月。
  • 目标价 Target： 预算 Motel $48–55/晚；中端酒店 $65–75/晚（≈$585–$675/月税前）。
  • 可接受上限 Max： $80/晚（≈$720/月税前；+HST≈$814/月）。超过即放弃该家。

两轮还价逻辑（按对方首报）

  • 对方报 $100 → 还 $70–75
  • 对方报 $90 → 还 $65–70
  • 对方报 $80 → 还 $60–70
  • 对方报 $70 → 接受（仍可顺手压到 $65）
  • 对方报 $60 以下（Motel 周价） → 直接接受并锁 13 周

杠杆：周二三为酒店周中相对低需夜；13 周 Guaranteed + 零周转成本 + 同房号 = 强谈判筹码。可承诺「先付押金/签简单协议」换更低锁价。

Negotiation anchors (never reveal your floor first)

  • Opening (Round 1): have them quote first for a "fixed 13-week, alternating Tuesday/Wednesday, same room number" corporate or long-stay rate. Never say $500/month first.
  • Target: budget motel $48–55/night; mid-range hotel $65–75/night (≈$585–$675/month pre-tax).
  • Max acceptable: $80/night (≈$720/month pre-tax; ≈$814/month with HST). Above that, walk away.

Two-round counter logic (based on their first quote)

  • They quote $100 → counter $70–75
  • They quote $90 → counter $65–70
  • They quote $80 → counter $60–70
  • They quote $70 → accept (you can still try for $65)
  • They quote $60 or less (motel weekly) → accept immediately and lock all 13 weeks

Leverage: Tuesdays and Wednesdays are relatively low-demand midweek nights; 13 guaranteed weeks + zero turnover cost + the same room number = strong bargaining chips. Offering "deposit now / simple agreement" can buy a lower locked rate.
