② Phase 1 · Before departure (1–3 months prior)

⏰ Window: 1–3 months before departure⏰ 时间窗口：出国前 1–3 个月 🎯 Land 1–2 weeks before classes (Fall 2026 starts 9/9)🎯 建议落地：早于开课 1–2 周（2026 秋季 9/9 开课）
📎 Documents (one digital copy + one printed copy each)
  • Passport (must be valid for the whole length of your studies)
  • LOA / letter of acceptance, tuition deposit receipt
  • Proof of funds: CAD bank statement or GIC certificate
  • Degrees & transcripts, vaccination records
  • Home driver's licence + certified translation (optional; useful for the G1 test or Photo ID)
  • 护照（有效期须覆盖整个学业长度）
  • LOA / 录取信、学费首付凭证
  • 资金证明：加元存款证明或 GIC 凭证
  • 学历与成绩单、疫苗记录
  • 本国驾照 + 国际翻译认证件（可选，落地考 G1 或办 Photo ID 用得上）
  • Confirm your admission and LOA (Letter of Acceptance); check that program / entry term / Co-op option match your actual plan.
  • Apply for a Study Permit and (if applicable) an entry visa; prepare proof of funds (CAD deposit / GIC).
  • Set up the GIC (if your program requires it) and the tuition deposit; keep every receipt and certificate (you may need them at the border / when opening accounts).
  • Book flights: arrive 1–2 weeks before term start to leave buffer for errands.
  • Arrange housing: on-campus residence / off-campus rental / temporary stay — see this toolkit's weekday temporary stay and locker map.
  • Make cloud backups of all documents: passport, LOA, proof of funds, vaccination records, degree files (clear names, two copies).
  • Set up an international credit card / carry a small amount of CAD cash for arrival emergencies.
  • 确认 录取与 LOA（Letter of Acceptance），核对专业 / 入学季 / Co-op 选项是否与你实际计划一致。
  • 申请 学习许可（Study Permit） 与（如适用）入境签证；准备 资金证明（加元存款 / GIC）。
  • 办理 GIC（如项目要求）与学费首付，保留所有收据与凭证（后续入境 / 开户可能用到）。
  • 预订机票：建议早于开学 1–2 周 抵达，留缓冲处理落地琐事。
  • 准备住宿：校内宿舍 / 校外租房 / 临时住宿——可参考本工具箱的 周中临时住宿 与 储物柜地图。
  • 整理 证件电子版云端备份：护照、录取信、资金证明、疫苗记录、学历文件（命名清晰、双份存储）。
  • 开通国际信用卡 / 准备少量 加元现金 落地应急。
Key reminder:关键提醒： your study permit must be valid for the whole length of your program; funds and GIC paperwork are commonly checked at the border and when opening local accounts — get them organised early. 学签有效期务必覆盖整个学业长度；资金与 GIC 材料是入境与本地开户的常见核查项，提前整理好。

③ Phase 2 · Arriving in Waterloo (within 1–2 weeks)

⏰ Window: within 1–2 weeks of landing⏰ 时间窗口：落地后 1–2 周内 🎓 Graduate orientation week: 2026-09-07 ~ 09-10🎓 研究生迎新周：2026-09-07 ~ 09-10
📎 Documents (reused constantly for accounts & IDs)
  • Passport + Study Permit (issued at the border — check the name spelling and expiry on the spot)
  • Proof of address: lease, or a utility / phone bill showing your name and address
  • UW LOA (frequently requested when opening a bank account or applying for a SIN)
  • A little CAD cash (taxi / meals on day one)
  • 护照 + Study Permit（入境当场领取，务必核对姓名拼写与有效期）
  • 住址证明：租房合同，或含本人姓名与地址的水电 / 话费账单
  • UW 录取信 / LOA（办银行卡、办 SIN 常被要求出示）
  • 加元现金少量（落地首日打车、餐费应急）
  • Border entry: go through with passport / study permit / LOA and collect your Study Permit — check the expiry and name spelling on the spot.
  • Phone plan: Bell / Rogers / Telus or a budget brand (Public Mobile / Fido / Chatr); can be set up right after landing — pick a plan with data.
  • Bank account: RBC / TD / Scotiabank / BMO / CIBC; international student accounts are often fee-free — bring passport + study permit + proof of address.
  • SIN (Social Insurance Number): required to work and to file taxes; apply online or in person at Service Canada and keep it safe.
  • UHIP health insurance: the mandatory international health plan shared by 22 Ontario public universities — $948/year for 2026–27 (3 instalments, $316 per term), charged automatically to your student account, no separate enrolment; coverage cap CAD 1,000,000/year. It only takes effect once your account shows "Fees Arranged" — so paying fees is not something to leave to the last minute.
  • Photo ID / driver's licence: with no local licence you can take the Ontario G1 test, or get an Ontario Photo ID with passport + proof of address.
  • Off-campus renters: set up internet / utilities (leases usually require the tenant to arrange these).
  • 机场入境： 持护照 / 学签 / LOA 走入境通道，当场领取 Study Permit——当场核对有效期与姓名拼写。
  • 手机卡： Bell / Rogers / Telus 或廉价品牌（Public Mobile / Fido / Chatr），落地即可办，选含流量的套餐。
  • 银行开户： RBC / TD / Scotiabank / BMO / CIBC；国际学生账户多免月费，带护照 + 学签 + 住址证明。
  • SIN（工号）： 打工 / 报税必需，Service Canada 线上或线下申请，拿到后妥善保存。
  • 医保 UHIP： 安省 22 所公立大学统一的国际生强制医保，2026–27 年度 $948/年（分 3 期，每学期 $316），自动计入学生账户、无需自行投保；保额上限 100 万加元/年。需账户状态变为 "Fees Arranged" 后才正式生效——所以缴费不是拖到最后一刻的事。
  • Photo ID / 驾照： 如无本国驾照，可考 Ontario G1；或用护照 + 住址证明办 Ontario Photo ID。
  • 校外租房者：办理 网络 / 水电 开户（租赁合同通常要求租客自行申请）。
Priority order:优先级建议： collect study permit at the border → phone → bank + SIN → UHIP → Photo ID. Those are the five highest-leverage things in your first few days. 入境拿学签 → 手机 → 银行+SIN → UHIP → Photo ID，是落地头几天最高杠杆的五件事。

④ Phase 3 · Enrolment (before–early term)

⏰ Window: late August – mid September⏰ 时间窗口：8 月下旬 – 9 月中 🚨 09-23 NFA freeze takes effect🚨 09-23 NFA 冻结生效 🚨 10-31 Fees Arranged deadline🚨 10-31 Fees Arranged 截止
📎 Documents / prerequisites
  • UW ID (the 8-digit student number on your LOA) — required to activate WatIAM
  • WatIAM account + @uwaterloo.ca email — the single entry point to every university system
  • WatCard student card: building access, library, on-campus spending, transit pass
  • Co-op students also need a Waterloo Works profile (resume + PD course requirements)
  • UW ID（录取信上的 8 位学号）—— 激活 WatIAM 必需
  • WatIAM 账号与 @uwaterloo.ca 邮箱 —— 所有学校系统的统一入口
  • WatCard 学生卡：门禁、图书馆、校内消费、公交一卡通
  • Co-op 学生另需 Waterloo Works 档案（简历 + PD 课程要求）
  • Activate your WatIAM account and UW email (@uwaterloo.ca) — the entry point to all university systems.
  • Use Quest to complete course registration: enrolling, adding / dropping, checking your timetable and prerequisites.
  • Co-op students: complete your profile in Waterloo Works and understand the PD (Professional Development) course requirements and timeline.
  • Pay tuition (Finance account): from 9/23 accounts without a payment arrangement are frozen with an NFA (which directly limits course selection), and 10/31 is the last day to arrange "Fees Arranged"; watch the CAD exchange rate and keep receipts.
  • Collect / activate your student card (WatCard) — building access, library and on-campus spending in one.
  • Attend Orientation / department welcome events, join student groups and build your information channels.
  • Get to know campus: library, IST (IT services), writing / English support, health centre.
  • 激活 WatIAM 账号 与 UW 邮箱（@uwaterloo.ca），这是所有学校系统的入口。
  • 用 Quest 完成课程注册：选课、加退课、查看 timetable 与 prerequisites。
  • Co-op 学生：在 Waterloo Works 完善 profile、了解 PD 课程（Professional Development）要求与时间线。
  • 缴纳 学费（Finance 账户）：9/23 起未做缴费安排的账户会被加 NFA 冻结（直接影响选课等操作），10/31 是办理 "Fees Arranged" 的最后期限；注意加元汇率并保留凭证。
  • 领取 / 激活 学生卡（WatCard）——门禁、图书馆、校内消费一体。
  • 参加 Orientation / 院系迎新，加入学生群与社团，建立信息渠道。
  • 熟悉 campus：图书馆、IST（IT 服务）、写作 / 英语辅导、健康中心。
Co-op timeline:Co-op 时间线： Waterloo's study terms and job search overlap heavily — plan course selection and recruiting together, or you may only discover the application window has closed mid-term. 滑铁卢的 study term 与 job search 高度交错，选课与求职要并行规划，避免学期中才发现投递窗口已过。

⑤ Phase 4 · Academic & daily life (ongoing)

⏰ Window: throughout your studies⏰ 时间窗口：贯穿整个学业 🚌 U-Pass: automatic for full-time students, tap your WatCard🚌 U-Pass：全日制本硕自动含，刷 WatCard 乘车
  • Course pace: Waterloo terms are short (~13 weeks) and fast — build a weekly plan early instead of cramming before midterms.
  • Co-op cycle: keep an eye on the job-search and study-term timeline, and prepare your resume / interviews early.
  • Campus resources: library, tutoring, counselling, career centre, health centre — all included in your tuition.
  • Transit: full-time undergraduate and graduate students automatically receive the GRT U-Pass (already included in incidental fees) and can tap their WatCard for unlimited rides on GRT buses and the ION light rail; without the card you pay a $3.75 cash fare and must have exact change. For Toronto commuting, GO Transit is an option.
  • Taxes: file with your SIN and tuition receipt (T2202, usually downloadable from your student account by late February); international students can also get part of their tax back.
  • Append any problem you hit to this page's pitfalls log so it becomes more and more your own onboarding encyclopaedia.
  • 课程节奏： 滑铁卢 term 短（约 13 周）、进度快，尽早建立每周计划，不要等 midterm 前才突击。
  • Co-op 周期： 关注 job search 与 study term 的时间线，提前准备简历 / 面试。
  • 校园资源： 图书馆、tutoring、心理咨询、职业中心、健康中心——都是已含在学费里的权益。
  • 交通： 全日制本科生与研究生都自动获得 GRT U-Pass（已含在 incidental fees 里），刷 WatCard 即可无限次乘坐 GRT 公交与 ION 轻轨；忘带卡需付现金 $3.75 且须自备零钱。多伦多通勤可选 GO Transit。
  • 税务： 凭 SIN 与学费凭证（T2202，通常在次年 2 月底前可从学生账户下载）报税，国际学生同样可退回部分税款。
  • 把遇到的问题随时追加到本页 踩坑记录，让它越来越像「你自己的入学百科」。

⑦ Co-op job-search timeline (ECE MEng Co-op)

⏰ Window: across the 16–20 month program⏰ 时间窗口：贯穿 16–20 个月学制 🎯 WIL 601 must be completed before the first work term🎯 首个工签前必须完成 WIL 601 🔁 The program must start and end with a study term🔁 学制首尾都必须是 study term
📎 Job-search preparation (ready before the first work term)
  • Waterloo Works profile: resume + cover-letter templates + uploaded transcript
  • WIL 601 Career Foundations completed (required in the study term before the first work term)
  • Work Term Report template, submitted at the end of the term
  • Waterloo Works 档案：简历 + 求职信模板 + 已上传成绩单
  • 完成 WIL 601 Career Foundations（首个工签前的 study term 必修）
  • 工签报告（Work Term Report）模板，期末提交
  • Program structure: ECE MEng Co-op requires 8 courses (at least 5 within ECE, at most 3 from the Faculties of Engineering / Math / Science), over 16–20 months. Minimum 1 work term, maximum 2 consecutive work terms (same employer).
  • Start / end constraint: the program must begin and end with a study term — the final term cannot be a work term. If you don't secure a work term you are moved into the regular MEng program.
  • Recruiting rhythm: apply for the next work term through Waterloo Works during a study term; interviews are usually at the Tatham Centre on campus or online. Roles cover Canada and overseas (including the US TN visa route).
  • WIL 601: must be completed in the study term before your first work term; it is the gateway into the job-search process.
  • Work Term Report (WTR): after each work term it must be submitted before the end of the first week of the next term (e.g. a Spring work term → report due before the first week of Fall).
  • Co-op term dates: work terms start with the Fall (Sep–Dec) / Winter (Jan–Apr) / Spring (May–Aug) terms; the exact dates depend on your employer. This toolkit's Daily Reminders includes work-term start dates.
  • 学制结构： ECE MEng Co-op 需修满 8 门课（其中 ≥5 门在 ECE 系内，最多 3 门来自工程 / 数学 / 科学学院），学制 16–20 个月。最少 1 个工签、最多 2 个连续工签（同一雇主）。
  • 首尾约束： 学制必须以 study term 开始、以 study term 结束——最后一个 term 不能是工签。没拿到工签会被转入 regular MEng 项目。
  • 求职节奏： 在 study term 内通过 Waterloo Works 投递下一阶段工签岗位，面试通常在校内 Tatham Centre 或线上；岗位覆盖加拿大本地与海外（含美国 TN 签证通道）。
  • WIL 601： 必须在首个工签之前的 study term 完成，是进入求职流程的门槛。
  • 工签报告（WTR）： 每个工签结束后，须于下一 term 第一周结束前提交（如 Spring 工签 → 报告在 Fall 第一周前交）。
  • Co-op 学期节点： 工签跟随 Fall（9–12 月）/ Winter（1–4 月）/ Spring（5–8 月）学期开始；具体以雇主安排为准。本工具箱「每日提醒」含 Co-op 实习学期开始日。
Key reminder:关键提醒： the work term is a highly competitive job search, not something the university allocates. Apply to too few roles or interview unprofessionally and you can be removed from the process; prepare your resume early, apply widely, and stay in touch with the Co-operative Education office. 工签是高度竞争的求职过程，不是学校保证分配。申请数量不足或面试表现不专业会被移出求职流程；务必早准备简历、多投递、保持与 Co-operative Education 办公室的沟通。

⑧ Housing & rental guide (Waterloo / Kitchener)

⏰ Window: start looking 1–2 months before you land⏰ 时间窗口：落地前 1–2 个月开始看房 💡 Co-op creates a large sublet market every 4 months💡 Co-op 每 4 个月有大量转租（sublet）
📎 Signing / move-in documents
  • Standard Form of Lease (the Ontario statutory template)
  • Last month's rent deposit (the only deposit allowed — see the red lines below)
  • Letter of acceptance / student card (some landlords or buildings ask for proof of enrolment)
  • Key deposit (limited to the actual cost of cutting keys)
  • 标准租约（Standard Form of Lease，安省法定模板）
  • 最后一个月房租押金（仅此一项，见下方红线）
  • 录取信 / 学生证（部分房东或公寓要求 proof of enrollment）
  • 钥匙押金（仅限实际配匙成本）
  • On-campus residence: traditional residence about $950–1,150/month (with meal plan), suite-style $1,100–1,300/month; graduate spots are limited, so apply early.
  • Private student buildings: Icon / Sage / King Street Towers and similar, about $1,000–1,400/month, 5–10 minutes' walk to campus, mostly furnished.
  • Off-campus shared housing: a room in a 3-bedroom house/townhouse about $700–1,000/month; 1-bedroom apartment $1,700–2,100/month; 2-bedroom $2,100–2,600/month (2026 KW market — confirm against live listings).
  • Sublet advantage: Waterloo co-op students leave for work terms every 4 months, creating plenty of furnished sublets — find below-market short leases through the UW Off-Campus Housing board and community groups.
  • Check rent control before signing: units first occupied after 2018-11-15 are not subject to the rent increase guideline (landlords may price freely and raise rent beyond the 2.1% cap) — ask directly before signing.
  • Notice to vacate: usually 60 days written notice (Form N9); during a fixed lease the landlord generally cannot raise the rent.
  • 校内宿舍： 传统宿舍约 $950–1,150/月（含餐），套间式 $1,100–1,300/月；研究生名额有限，建议尽早申请。
  • 私营学生公寓： Icon / Sage / King Street Towers 等，约 $1,000–1,400/月，步行 5–10 分钟到校园，多为带家具。
  • 校外合租： 3 卧 house/townhouse 单间约 $700–1,000/月；1 卧公寓 $1,700–2,100/月；2 卧 $2,100–2,600/月（2026 年 KW 行情，具体以实时挂牌为准）。
  • Co-op 转租红利： 滑铁卢 co-op 学生每 4 个月离校实习，产生大量 furnished sublet，可通过 UW Off-Campus Housing board 与社群找到低于市场的短租。
  • 签约前核实租金管制： 2018-11-15 之后首次入住的单位不受租金指导线约束（房东可自由定价、涨租不受 2.1% 上限限制），签约前直接问清。
  • 退租通知： 通常需提前 60 天 书面通知（Form N9）；租约期内房东一般不能涨租。
Ontario rental red lines (Residential Tenancies Act, 2006):安省租房红线（Residential Tenancies Act, 2006）： a landlord may only collect a "last month's rent" deposit, and it may not exceed one month's rent; damage / pet / security deposits cannot be charged (they are illegal in Ontario). A key deposit is limited to the actual cost of cutting keys. Landlords must pay interest on the deposit each year at the guideline rate (about 2.1% in 2026). Post-dated cheques cannot be required. 房东只能收「最后一个月房租」押金，且不超过一个月房租；不能收 damage / pet / 安全押金（这些在安省违法）。钥匙押金仅限实际配匙成本。房东须每年按指导线利率（2026 约 2.1%）支付押金利息。不得强制要求 post-dated cheque。

⑨ Course-planning strategy (ECE MEng Co-op)

⏰ Window: select courses before term start, take them in study terms⏰ 时间窗口：开学前选课，study term 修课 🚨 Dropped in week 3 if fees are not arranged🚨 未 Fees Arranged 第 3 周被退课
📎 Before selecting courses
  • Quest account activated and able to log in
  • Your program plan: how the 8 courses are distributed
  • Co-op sequence: confirmed study / work term layout
  • Quest 账号已激活，能登录选课
  • 个人培养计划（program plan）：8 门课的分布
  • Co-op sequence：确认 study / work term 排布
  • Credit structure: 8 courses in total (0.5 credit each), of which at least 5 must be within ECE, and at most 3 may come from the Faculties of Engineering / Math / Science; you need ≥65% in each course and a ≥70% cumulative average to stay in the program.
  • Align with Co-op: courses can only be taken in study terms — when planning the sequence, leave enough study terms to finish all 8 courses plus a final study term (the program must both start and end with a study term).
  • Select early: lock in your courses as soon as Quest opens — popular courses fill up first; watch the prerequisites.
  • Linked to payment: graduate students who have not arranged fees are dropped by the system at the end of week 3; and from 9/23 an NFA freeze blocks course-selection actions (see "Phase 3 · Enrolment" and "Key dates at a glance").
  • Specialization: optional specializations have different required-course counts — check the relevant course list before selecting; specializations are not guaranteed and depend on what runs.
  • Overall cap: the whole degree allows at most 10 courses (including failures and audits); once you have completed 8 you cannot take extra courses.
  • 学分结构： 共需 8 门课（每门 0.5 学分），其中 ≥5 门在 ECE 系内，最多 3 门可来自工程 / 数学 / 科学学院；单科 ≥65%、累计均分 ≥70% 才能留项目。
  • 与 Co-op 对齐： 课程只能在 study term 修；规划 sequence 时预留足够 study term 修完 8 门课 + 一个收尾 study term（首尾都必须是 study term）。
  • 尽早选课： Quest 选课期开放后尽早锁定，热门课先满；注意 prerequisites。
  • 缴费联动： 研究生若未办 Fees Arranged，第 3 周结束会被系统退课；同时 9/23 起 NFA 冻结影响选课操作（详见「阶段三 · 入学注册」与「重要日期速查」）。
  • 方向（Specialization）： 可选专攻方向，各方向必修课数量不同，选课前先查对应课程清单；方向不保证、视开课情况而定。
  • 总量上限： 整个学位最多修 10 门课（含不及格与 audit），修满 8 门后不可再修额外课。
Priority order:优先级建议： sort out WIL 601 and your first study term's courses first so you can enter recruiting smoothly; then spread the remaining courses according to prerequisites and interest, so you don't end up short of credits in your final term. 先把 WIL 601 与第 1 个 study term 的课排好，确保能顺利进入求职；再按 prerequisites 与兴趣铺开剩余课程，避免最后一学期凑不齐学分。

⑩ Graduate fee breakdown (MEng · int'l reference)

⏰ Window: fees due at the start of each term⏰ 时间窗口：每学期初缴费 🚨 10-31 Fees Arranged deadline🚨 10-31 Fees Arranged 截止
📎 Payment essentials
  • Quest statement (tuition + incidental fee breakdown)
  • Promissory Note (instalment / deferred payment request)
  • A CAD account (allow 3–5 business days for cross-border transfers to land)
  • Quest 账单（学费 + 杂费明细）
  • Promissory Note（分期 / 暂缓缴费申请）
  • 加元账户（跨境汇款预留 3–5 个工作日到账）
  • Tuition: international ECE MEng students are charged per course; the whole degree (8 courses) is roughly CAD 50,000–60,000 (varies with the year and exchange rate — treat the official Tuition Estimator as authoritative). MEng has no scholarships and no supervisor funding; it is self-funded.
  • UHIP health insurance: $948/year (3 instalments, $316 per term), charged automatically; effective only once the account shows "Fees Arranged" (see "Phase 2 · Arrival").
  • U-Pass (GRT transit): automatic for full-time students, included in incidental fees, tap your WatCard to ride (see "Phase 4 · Academic life").
  • Other incidental fees: student services, athletics / clubs, facilities and so on are charged each term — see your Quest statement.
  • Refund dates: 100% refund deadline 9/29, 50% refund deadline 10/23; NFA freeze from 9/23 and the 10/31 Fees Arranged deadline (see "Key dates at a glance").
  • Offset with Co-op income: work terms are paid and can significantly offset living costs (engineering monthly salaries commonly CAD 3,500–5,000), though a work term may still carry a small amount of tuition.
  • 学费（Tuition）： ECE MEng 国际生按每门课计费，整段学位（8 门）约 CAD 50,000–60,000（随年份与汇率波动，以官方 Tuition Estimator 为准）。MEng 无奖学金、无导师资助，需自费。
  • UHIP 医保： $948/年（分 3 期，每学期 $316），自动计入账户；须账户变 "Fees Arranged" 后生效（详见「阶段二 · 抵达」）。
  • U-Pass（GRT 公交）： 全日制本硕自动含，已含在 incidental fees，刷 WatCard 乘车（详见「阶段四 · 学术生活」）。
  • 其它杂费： 学生服务费、体育 / 俱乐部费、设施费等每 term 另计，具体见 Quest 账单。
  • 退费节点： 100% 退费截止 9/29，50% 退费截止 10/23；9/23 起 NFA 冻结、10/31 Fees Arranged 最后期限（详见「重要日期速查」）。
  • Co-op 收入对冲： 工签为带薪，可显著抵消生活成本（工程类月薪常见 CAD 3,500–5,000），但工签 term 仍可能计少量学费。
Budget basis:预算口径： total spend ≈ tuition + incidental fees + living costs (shared housing in KW with home cooking, about CAD 15,000–22,000/year). For a study permit, international students must show they can fund first-year tuition plus roughly CAD 20,000 of living costs. 总支出 ≈ 学费 + 杂费 + 生活费（KW 合租 + 自炊约 CAD 15,000–22,000/年）。国际生学签需证明「首年学费 + 约 CAD 20,000 生活费」的资金能力。
